THE ESSENTIALS

A little more about the place

2 survey responses

In the heart of Collegetown, 305 Eddy Street is set back from the street and also accessible from Dryden Road (across from Aladdin’s) and Catherine Street. The landlord describes large bedrooms, a spacious common room, wide hallways, plenty of windows, and a private on-site laundromat. The building has eight apartments and houses about 30 students.
